Read CLAUDE.md. Run npm run sponsorship -- fulfilment. Use --json when you need to work further with the rows. Never invent a record, a date, an amount or a delivery that is not in the records.

Owed after the deal ended comes first, then past its due date, then behind. Behind means delivered share trails the share of the term gone by more than the tolerance in settings.
